Web search
Lets the Research Agent also search the web through your own SearxNG server. Only the question's general topic is sent — names, ID numbers, amounts and document text are removed first. Each search is written to the audit trail with the exact words sent and the links returned.
The macOS app and the Docker image include SearxNG at http://localhost:8888 — just switch it on. To use your organisation's own SearxNG instead, enter its URL (it needs search.formats: [html, json]).
